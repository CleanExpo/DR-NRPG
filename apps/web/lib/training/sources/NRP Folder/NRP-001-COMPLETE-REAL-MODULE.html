📝 Professional Assessment (10 Questions)

Complete this assessment to demonstrate your understanding of NRPG membership requirements. Passing score: 80%

Question 1: What is the minimum public liability insurance requirement for NRPG membership?

Explanation: NRPG requires minimum $20M public liability insurance to protect both contractors and clients. This aligns with industry standards and ensures adequate coverage for major commercial projects.

Question 2: Which IICRC certification is the minimum requirement for water damage restoration?

Explanation: WRT (Water Damage Restoration) is the foundational IICRC certification covering water damage principles, classification, and restoration procedures. It's prerequisite for advanced certifications.

Question 3: How often must moisture meters be calibrated for NRPG compliance?

Explanation: Annual calibration ensures measurement accuracy and meets insurance requirements. Calibration must be performed by certified technicians with traceable standards.

Question 4: What is the validity period for a National Police Check for NRPG membership?

Explanation: Police checks must be obtained within 12 months of application to ensure current criminal history status. This protects clients and maintains professional standards.

Question 5: Which Australian standard covers respiratory protective equipment selection?

Explanation: AS/NZS 1715:2009 specifies selection, use, and maintenance of respiratory protective equipment. Critical for hazardous material work in restoration.
