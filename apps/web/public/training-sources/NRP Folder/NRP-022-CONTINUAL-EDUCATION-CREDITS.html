🎓 NRP-022: Continuing Professional Development (CPD)

Keep Your Training Current, Keep Good Records & Check Renewal Rules

Duration: 35 minutes | Assessment: 10 questions | Category: Professional Development

📋 Module Overview

This module covers continuing professional development (CPD): why restoration professionals keep their training current, how to build CPD into your working year, how to keep records that stand up to checking, and how to confirm the renewal rules of any body whose certificate you hold.

NRPG does not set renewal rules for other organisations. Each certifying body sets and changes its own rules, so you are responsible for checking them directly with that body.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Explain why continuing training matters for safe, current restoration practice
  • Identify practical ways to build CPD into your working year
  • Use NRPG training modules as part of your CPD
  • Keep clear CPD records that you can produce when asked
  • Check the renewal rules of any body whose certificate you hold

📚 Why Keep Your Training Current

Methods, equipment, materials and standards change. Training you completed some years ago may no longer match current good practice, and the people relying on your work (property owners, insurers and your own team) expect you to know what is current.

📖 What current training protects

Safety: new hazards, products and controls are understood before they are used on site
Quality: work follows current methods and manufacturer instructions
Documentation: reports and records meet what insurers and clients now expect
Credibility: you can show what you have learned and when

💡 Ways to Build Your CPD

1. NRPG Training Modules

Completing NRPG training modules is a practical way to keep your knowledge current. Choose modules that match the work you do and the gaps you have found in your own practice, and record each module you complete, with the date.

2. Webinars, Workshops and Events

Industry webinars, workshops and events keep you up to date on new techniques, changes to standards and case studies from other contractors. Keep the attendance confirmation or certificate where one is issued.

3. Manufacturer and Supplier Training

Equipment manufacturers and chemical suppliers often provide training on correct use, maintenance and safety. This training supports safe use of the products you work with. Keep a record of what was covered.

4. Learning From Your Own Jobs

Review completed jobs with your team. Note what worked, what did not, and what you would change. Sharing what you learn with newer team members is also part of building a professional practice.

📊 Keeping CPD Records

A clear record shows what you learned, when and from whom. Keep it in a form you control, such as a folder or a spreadsheet, and update it soon after each activity.

What to Record

  • Date of the activity
  • Provider (for example NRPG, a supplier, an industry body)
  • Topic and a short note of what you learned
  • Time spent, in hours
  • Evidence: certificate, attendance confirmation, module completion or notes

📅 Review Regularly

Look over your record at least once a quarter. Check whether it covers the work you actually do, and plan the next activity to fill any gaps.

🔄 Checking Renewal Rules of Any Body Whose Certificate You Hold

If you hold a certificate from an industry body, a training provider or a regulator, that organisation decides what is needed to keep it valid. Do not rely on a summary written by someone else, including this module.

What to Check With the Issuing Body

Question Why it matters
Does the certificate expire or need renewing? You can plan ahead and avoid a lapse
What training or evidence is required for renewal? You can match your CPD activities to the requirement
Which providers and activities does the body accept? You avoid spending time on activities that will not be recognised
How and when must you submit your records? You meet the deadline and the correct format
What fees apply, and what happens if the certificate lapses? You understand the cost and the consequences before they arise

⚠️ Confirm in Writing

Rules change. Check the issuing body's current published requirements, or ask them directly, and keep a copy of the answer with your CPD record.

🎯 Good Habits and Mistakes to Avoid

Good Habits

  1. Spread your learning across the year rather than leaving it to the last month
  2. Mix learning sources: modules, webinars, workshops and supplier training
  3. Record each activity straight away, while the details are fresh
  4. Keep certificates and confirmations in one place
  5. Set calendar reminders for the renewal dates of each certificate you hold
  6. Update your NRPG profile when you gain or renew a qualification

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ❌ Leaving all of your learning until just before a renewal date
  • ❌ Assuming an activity will be recognised without checking with the issuing body
  • ❌ Not keeping certificates or notes of what you completed
  • ❌ Relying on a summary of someone else's rules instead of the body's own current requirements
  • ❌ Letting a profile show a qualification that has expired

📖 Key Takeaways

  • Keeping your training current protects safety, quality and your reputation
  • NRPG modules, events, supplier training and job reviews can all form part of your CPD
  • Keep dated records with evidence of everything you complete
  • Each certifying body sets its own renewal rules, so check them directly
  • Spread your learning across the year and review your record regularly
