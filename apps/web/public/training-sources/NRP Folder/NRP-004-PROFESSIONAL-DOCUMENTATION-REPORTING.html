1. Documentation Standards & Requirements

Legal Requirement: All restoration work must be documented according to Australian Standards AS/NZS 3666:2011 and insurance company requirements. Inadequate documentation can void insurance claims and result in legal liability.
Document Type Standard/Requirement Completion Timeline Retention Period Legal Status
Initial Assessment Report IICRC S500/S520 standards Within 24 hours 7 years Legally required
Scope of Works RestoreAssist.ai industry standard Before work commencement Project completion + 7 years Contract requirement
Daily Progress Reports Insurer specific templates End of each work day Project completion + 2 years Contract requirement
Completion Certificate AS/NZS 3666:2011 Project handover Warranty period + 7 years Legally required
Warranty Documentation Australian Consumer Law Project handover Warranty period + 7 years Legally required

Initial Assessment Template

Purpose: First on-site evaluation and damage documentation

Sections: Property details, damage extent, cause identification, safety concerns, preliminary scope

Completion Time: 30-45 minutes on-site

Scope of Works Template

Purpose: Detailed work plan and cost estimation

Sections: Materials list, labor requirements, timeline, equipment needs, compliance requirements

Completion Time: 2-4 hours preparation

Progress Report Template

Purpose: Daily work documentation and milestone tracking

Sections: Work completed, challenges encountered, next day plans, photo documentation

Completion Time: 10-15 minutes daily

Completion Certificate

Purpose: Final handover and compliance verification

Sections: Work summary, standards compliance, quality verification, warranty terms

Completion Time: 30 minutes final inspection
